List 150 categories such that a person could be asked to name one member of the category ("name a ___"). Anything goes: objects, living things, substances, places, natural phenomena, events, historical periods, activities, works of art, music or literature, fictional things, feelings, ideas, units, sounds, smells, and so on. Any size of category is fine, from a handful of members to thousands; mix the familiar and the specialised.
For each one give the category, the answer most people would give first, and one or more other likely answers.
Avoid categories of people (names, nationalities, ethnicities, religions, genders) and anything about computers, software, programming, math or AI.{avoid}

Format: one category per line, numbered, pipe-separated, no headings, no commentary:
N. category | most common answer | other likely answers
